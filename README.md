# Phone Factory

A CoyoTech production — a Turbo Pizza-style factory game: assemble iPhones
on the line. Juggle three orders at once, each with its own patience clock;
start timed stations (Test, Press, Cure) and leave them to cook while you
work another phone; carry two parts at once; chain shipments to the truck
for bonus pay; spend credits on upgrades between generations.

- **Gen 1:** 3–4 parts, no waiting. **Gen 2:** 3–5 parts, max 1 waiting.
  **Gen 3:** 4–5 parts, max 2 waiting. **Gen 4:** 4–6 parts, max 2 waiting.
  **Gen 5 (max difficulty):** 5–6 parts, max 3 waiting.
- Miss a deadline and the order goes late: reduced pay during a grace
  period, then the order is cancelled.
- In Gen 1 the Frame brings its Screen + Battery with it; the 3D phone
  builds in X-ray view during assembly and goes solid when fully assembled.

Play it at https://ckeisc.github.io/phone-factory/ — installable as a PWA
(Add to Home Screen). Single self-contained `index.html` plus `assets/`;
three.js loads from CDN.

Icon is provisional — a panicking worker putting parts on an iPhone, in the
game's art style — and will be swapped for the final icon.
